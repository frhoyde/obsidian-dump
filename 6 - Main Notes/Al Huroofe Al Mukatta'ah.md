# Al Huroofe Al Mukatta'ah

> Groups o f letters (al-huroof al-muqatta ‘ah) appear at the beginning o f several surahs. The most correct scholarly view states that we are required to believe in them as they appear to be, without delving into their meaning, knowledge o f which we should leave to Allah. We believe that they were not revealed in vain, and that there is wisdom behind them o f which we are not aware.
>  - Tafseer As Sadi
