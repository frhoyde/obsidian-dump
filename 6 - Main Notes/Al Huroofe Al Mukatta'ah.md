# Al Huroofe Al Mukatta'ah

> Groups o f letters (al-huroof al-muqatta ‘ah) appear at the beginning o f several surahs. The most correct scholarly view states that we are required to believe in them as they appear to be, without delving into their meaning, knowledge o f which we should leave to Allah. We believe that they were not revealed in vain, and that there is wisdom behind them o f which we are not aware.
>  - Tafseer As Sadi

So the most authentic opinion about these letters is that Allah knows best. Whatever we can derive from the wisdom should be returned to Allah and it should be always mentioned that Allah Knows best.

> A group of scholars, such as the Rightly-Guided Khaleefahs Radiallahu ta'ala Anhum, and others among the Sahaabah, Taabi’een and their followers, refrained from interpreting these aayahs. It was not narrated that the Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) interpreted them, so it is preferable for us to say Allah knows best what they mean. But some of the mufassireen did interpret these to the best of their abilities. 
> 
> Some of the scholars tried to discover the wisdom behind these letters and said: These letters are mentioned – and Allah knows best – at the beginning of surahs which point to the miraculous nature of the Qur’aan, which implies that all mankind is unable to match it, even though it is composed of the letters that they use in their daily speech. This was the view supported by Shaykh al-Islam Ibn Taymiyah Rahimahullah and was approved of by Abu’l-Hajjaaj al-Mazzi Rahimahullah.  [[#^189898]]
